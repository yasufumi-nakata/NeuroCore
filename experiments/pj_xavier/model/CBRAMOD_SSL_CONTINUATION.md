# Additional unlabeled EEG continuation

This experiment expands the amount of training-eligible EEG used by the model before the same fixed observed-action alignment. It is separate from every previously trained checkpoint. The actual retained waveform duration, completed epochs, and evaluated performance are recorded in measured reports; the candidate raw hours below are not a claim that training has finished.

## Data and objective fixed before training

The raw candidate inventory contains 18 fixed UniBa training participants (15.991899 hours, rounded) and the portable dataset's 19 participants across three separate device sessions (34.897061 hours). The combined 50.888960 candidate hours include the portable validation participants and windows later rejected by quality checks. All 14 UniBa validation/test participants, both UniBa clock-rejected recordings, and all 26 ITU recordings are explicitly excluded from this continuation. These 42 denied binary source hashes supplement the positive 75-record allowlist.

Portable participants are ordered by SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string consisting of 20261122, a vertical bar, and their canonical participant group. The first 15 participants form SSL training and the last four form reconstruction validation. All three devices of one person stay in one partition. UniBa uses only the previously fixed 18 training people. The quality-independent candidate grid has 5,168 training and 872 validation windows of 30 seconds each; actual admission is decided by the fixed quality rule.

Each recording retains its actual EEG montage, with named channel order: eight UniBa EEG channels or 32 portable EEG channels. The portable montage lacks six of the existing alignment model's eight channels, so no channel is invented or padded. Training batches contain a single actual channel shape.

The reader's physical header scale converts raw samples to volts. The UniBa BIDS channel-table unit disagrees with the BrainVision header; both declarations are preserved in the source inventory. Fresh MNE decoding and an independent header conversion matched all 1,433,600 values of the sealed 400 UniBa training windows. Portable raw signals receive an offline fourth-order 3–40 Hz Butterworth SOS forward-backward filter; UniBa retains its already distributed filtered signal without another bandpass. Each recording excludes its first and last five seconds. The remaining fixed 30-second grid has stride 30 seconds, then each window is resampled to 200 Hz with polyphase resampling and fixed constant padding.

After resampling, all samples must be finite, every channel must have standard deviation at least 0.01 microvolt, and every absolute sample must be at most 100 microvolts. The saved float32 array is volts multiplied by 10,000, equivalent to the official CBraMod microvolt/100 scale. There is no window z-score or clipping. Every array slot has exactly one integer, in-range provenance row; every original source interval is nonoverlapping. The independent preflight reads every array byte, every window, and both real channel shapes before GPU admission.

The official model has 4,924,000 parameters including its reconstruction head. All except the original 200-element zero mask encoding are trainable. Exactly half of each example's channel-by-one-second patches are masked. Mean squared error is computed only on masked raw samples. Training masks are random; validation masks are fixed by record ID and seed. The selected encoder minimizes the same portable validation reconstruction loss across epochs, with the earliest checkpoint winning ties. Behavior labels and environment text are not SSL inputs.

The model definition and initial weights are pinned to the official [CBraMod repository](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/tree/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a) and recorded weight SHA-256. This is additional adaptation using its masked reconstruction mechanism, with an explicit new corpus, learning rate, preprocessing, and budget. It does not claim to reproduce the original pretraining run. The exclusion audit covers this continuation; it does not independently re-audit every source used in the original upstream pretraining.

## Bounded execution and unchanged alignment

The registered SSL budget is at most ten epochs and 1,800 seconds of loop/validation time, AdamW learning rate 0.0001, weight decay 0.05, gradient clipping at one, and seed 20261122. Parameter and FFT-input tensors stay float32; CUDA operations use bfloat16 autocast where supported. This avoids asking the length-200 FFT to run in unsupported half precision. Memory is limited to 21 GiB.

Capacity is measured in fresh processes for batches 8, 16, 32, and 64, for 50 train-only steps per candidate, alternating real 32- and eight-channel inputs. The largest tested stable candidate within budget is selected. All probe weights are discarded; the actual continuation restarts from the official weights and fixed seed. The main model records exact optimizer state, RNG state, next-batch cursor, unique source windows seen, and repeated input seconds for a faithful resume.

The subsequent alignment keeps the previous CBraMod symbol experiment's settings: the same 400 training windows, their final one second, physical amplitude scaling, eight prefix tokens, A/B/C single-token mapping, frozen SmolLM2-1.7B, fresh projection, batch 32, 30 epochs or 900 seconds, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.01, and the same seed. Only the frozen encoder's selected weights and their provenance change. A separate batch-32 train-only capacity check must pass. Both the adapted encoder and the language model remain frozen during this alignment.

The selected SSL checkpoint is sealed by comparing its actual tensors with the exported encoder and by verifying the minimum validation-loss history entry. An immutable encoder seal is then embedded into the new alignment checkpoint. Evaluation uses the same five conditions and frozen held-out cohorts, and the external runner separately checks that ITU source hashes never entered SSL. This is an exploratory reuse of the UniBa test cohort. The natural-use precision and autonomous-agent readiness remain separate, unproven quantities.

## Files and handoff

The scientific modules are cbramod_ssl.py and cbramod_ssl_token_llm.py. verify_ssl_corpus.py performs the full CPU corpus preflight. run_ssl_workflow.py runs capacity, SSL, encoder sealing, fixed alignment, and the five-condition evaluation sequentially, retaining each command, PID, exit code, log hash, and failure. It refuses to silently repeat a prior stage. Canonical evaluation publication stays pending until independent scoring/recovery and the next external-site gate are ready.

The workflow is launched through the workstation Colab CLI. It uses the existing session's earlier deadline, 04:20:34 UTC on 2026-11-22, without extension. Its initial admission requires at least 70 minutes remaining; the main SSL stage additionally reserves at least 60 minutes. Actual CU balance, disk reserve, GPU idleness, and source seals are checked again before launch. A step or final validation/save may cross the inner training timer check, so receipts report actual elapsed time rather than silently equating it with the configured budget.

Seventeen focused CPU tests passed on the workstation, including the official full encoder on both channel shapes, masked loss, deterministic validation masking, unique array slots, optimizer/RNG restore, preservation of an earlier best after resume, exact alignment logits at identical weights, and a disclosed synthetic weight-delta loader fixture. These CPU fixtures establish execution and provenance behavior, not useful EEG decoding performance. The separate real-corpus preflight adds two true training-waveform gradients before GPU training.

## Completed corpus admission before GPU training

All 75 allowed source records were processed in 196.484 seconds with the preregistered quality rule unchanged. Of 6,040 candidate windows, 2,200 were retained: training 1,678 windows / 50,340 unique source seconds / 13.983333 hours, and reconstruction validation 522 windows / 15,660 seconds / 4.35 hours. The 166 prepared arrays contain 1,685,013,248 file bytes. Source time is not multiplied by channel count or by repeated epochs.

The admitted data are highly imbalanced by source domain. UniBa contributes only eight training windows (240 seconds) from three of its 18 eligible training participants. The other 2,192 windows are portable-task EEG, including all 522 validation windows. Thus this continuation primarily adapts the encoder to portable experimental EEG; it is not bulk learning from 14 hours of natural coding EEG. The original quality criterion remains fixed rather than being relaxed after observing this coverage.

A separate model-side preflight rehashed every prepared array and checked every one of 421,248,000 float32 values, source exclusions, participant partitions, nonoverlapping raw intervals, and exact one-row-per-array-slot correspondence. It found maximum absolute amplitude 99.955428 microvolts and minimum channel standard deviation 0.390652 microvolts. Two true training windows, one per channel shape, produced finite nonzero CPU encoder gradients; those diagnostic updates were discarded. This establishes that the admitted input can drive learning, and does not establish useful intent decoding.

The continuation training protocol is sealed as cbramod_ssl_training_protocol.json, SHA-256 9f264097ae6699fa4320e89afc26a5eac999bee0113788325c6001f6d249a8b0. The per-release participant grouping and raw-source exclusions are auditable; anonymous physical participant identity across separate public releases is not independently linkable.
